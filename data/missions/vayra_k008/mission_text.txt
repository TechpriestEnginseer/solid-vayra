Location: Yakchal, Mirage system
Date: AI War Interbellum

"What I remember about the Harrowing was how quiet it was. During the Caliph's finalization, military members loyal to the Insurgency disappeared in an instant. Were there any doubts from the Theocrats? Any second thoughts? Perhaps, but none said a word. Watchers forgive us."

Hazqiyal, instrumental to the Caliph restoration, was politically disgraced and charged with harboring political and religious dissidents from his high office. When his services were no longer required, Hazqiyal and his seditious associates were sentenced to exile. Unwilling to risk further bloodshed, Hazqiyal acquiesced.
